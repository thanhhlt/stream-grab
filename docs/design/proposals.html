StreamGrab popup — design proposals (November 2026)

Chosen: 1 · Claw (2026-11-09), specified in docs/DESIGN.md. Directions 2 and 3 are kept as alternatives, described in ALTERNATIVES.md. Each direction shows Detected, Queue and the empty state. Press Download in a mockup to play its signature animation; Light/Dark switches the color mode. Sample titles are in several languages on purpose; the UI text stays as it is in the extension. Fonts load from Google Fonts here only; the extension bundles them.

1 · Claw (chosen)

playful, memorable, tied to the word “Grab”

The logo is a small arcade claw. On Download the video tile flies up, the claw drops, grabs it and drops it on the Queue tab, whose count bumps: the motion shows where the video went. The empty page shows the claw swinging while it waits. Bricolage Grotesque, pill buttons, tangerine.

2 · Current (alternative)

calm, soft, reassuring

A stream is water. Progress is a tube of liquid whose wave edge moves while data arrives and stops, turning amber, when the download stalls, so anyone can tell whether it is still working. Merging shows two streams (picture and sound) joining into one. The empty page shows ripples. Lexend, a typeface designed for easier reading.

3 · Ticket (alternative)

tactile, witty, a cinema metaphor

Each video is a ticket whose tear-off stub holds the quality and Download. On Download the stub tears along the perforation and flies to Queue while the ticket gets a “Queued” stamp; queued tickets have a torn edge and saved files get a “Saved” stamp. The empty page prints a blank ticket from a slot. Archivo with its width axis (condensed numbers on the stub).
