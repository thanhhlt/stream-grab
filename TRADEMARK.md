1. Use a different name and a different icon. Do not use "StreamGrab" in the extension name,

2. Keep the copyright notices and say that your version is modified, with the date (GPL-3.0

3. Do not present your version as official, endorsed by or published by the original author.
4. Your own donation or support links must be your own, and must not appear under the
   original name.

linked from [README.md](README.md). Anything else is a fork.

Copies that use the name or icon of the original may be reported to the store as impersonation.
